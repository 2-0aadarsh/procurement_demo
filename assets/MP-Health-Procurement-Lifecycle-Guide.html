Vendor Lifecycle — 12 Stages (Registration to Closure)

Stage 1

Registration

Submit company profile and mandatory registration documents on the vendor portal.

  • Company name, address, contact details
  • GSTIN, PAN, CIN (if applicable)
  • Category selection: Drugs / Equipment / Services / Consumables
  • Upload incorporation certificate & authorized signatory proof
Tip: Ensure GSTIN matches bank account name exactly — mismatches delay KYC.
Stage 2

KYC & Bank Verification

Complete identity verification and bank account validation.

  • Upload cancelled cheque or bank verification letter
  • Drug license / ISO / CE certificates as per category
  • Digital signature registration (if required)
  • Respond to verification queries within 48 hours
Stage 3

Vendor Code Generation

System assigns unique vendor ID (e.g. VND-MP-000123) after successful KYC.

  • Verify vendor code on dashboard
  • Link code to all future bids and contracts
  • Update profile if business details change
Stage 4

Eligibility Validation

Confirm you meet tender-specific criteria before bidding.

  • Minimum turnover & experience requirements
  • Blacklist / debarment check
  • Category-wise certifications (WHO-GMP, ISO 13485, etc.)
  • Build compliance matrix against NIT/RFP
Stage 5

Bid Submission

Prepare and submit technical & financial bids before deadline.

  • Technical bid: specs compliance, catalogues, test reports
  • Financial bid: sealed separately until opening stage
  • EMD payment (DD / e-BG) — mandatory for valid bid
  • Confirm portal submission receipt before deadline
Tip: You can revisit Stages 1–4 to update documents if your profile changed before bid submission.
Stage 6

Evaluation

Government committee evaluates technical and commercial bids.

  • Respond to clarification requests promptly
  • Attend technical presentation if scheduled
  • Commercial bid opened only for technically qualified vendors
Stage 7

Award & Contract

Receive Letter of Award (LOA) and execute contract.

  • Acknowledge LOA within stipulated period
  • Submit Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG) 5–10% of contract value
  • Sign contract with delivery schedule & SLA terms
Stage 8

Delivery

Dispatch goods with complete documentation.

  • Delivery challan with batch / serial numbers
  • Expiry dates (minimum shelf life as per contract)
  • Cold chain logs for vaccines / biologics
Stage 9

Quality & SLA

Meet quality standards and service level agreements.

  • Support inspection & sampling at delivery
  • Rectify quality observations within timeline
  • AMC / warranty obligations for equipment
Stage 10

Invoice & Payment

Raise invoice after acceptance and track payment.

  • Invoice with GRN reference & delivery proof
  • Three-way match: PO, GRN, Invoice
  • Track payment status on portal
Stage 11

Performance Score

Weighted score across quality, lead time, cost, regulatory & satisfaction.

  • Quality (30%), Lead Time (20%), Cost (20%)
  • Regulatory (20%), User Satisfaction (10%)
  • Preferred vendor status at score ≥ 85
Stage 12

Renewal / Closure

Contract renewal, re-tender, or formal closure.

  • Release of PBG after defect liability period
  • No-dues certificate & audit trail archive
  • Re-register for new tender cycles if needed

Government Lifecycle — 13 Stages (Need Identification to Payment)

Stage 1

Need Identification

Assess stock, patient load, disease burden and gap analysis.

Stage 2

Indent Raised

Store manager raises indent when stock unavailable.

Stage 3

Stock Check

Verify warehouse, inter-facility stock and open POs.

Stage 4

Demand Consolidation

Consolidate district requirements; optimize from existing stock.

Stage 5

PR & Budget Approval

Purchase requisition with budget head and financial sanction.

Stage 6

Tender Preparation

Draft NIT/RFP with BOQ, eligibility, EMD and evaluation method.

Stage 7

Contract Approval

Contract must be approved before PO generation (policy gate).

Stage 8

Bid Evaluation

Technical + financial evaluation using L1 or QCBS.

Stage 9

Award

Issue LOA, obtain PBG, sign formal contract.

Stage 10

Purchase Order

Generate PO after contract execution.

Stage 11

GRN & Inspection

Goods receipt, QC testing and acceptance certificate.

Stage 12

Invoice Matching

Three-way match: PO, GRN, Invoice.

Stage 13

Payment

Process payment; apply liquidated damages if applicable.
